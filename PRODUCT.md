## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (no framework), deployed on Cloudflare Pages. Source repo: https://github.com/joaogks/palestra.git

## Users

- Owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses (PMEs) in Toledo – PR and the western Paraná region, deciding whether AI is worth their team's time and money.
- HR and team leads who hire training for their staff.
- Business associations (ACI, CDL, Sebrae and similar) that program talks and events for members.

Their job on this site: understand who João is, believe he can make AI practical for their business, and contact him to book a talk, workshop or diagnosis.

## Product Purpose

João Signor offers talks, hands-on workshops and implementation help on applied AI for companies. The site exists to turn a visitor into a WhatsApp conversation about a booking. Success = contact initiated.

## Positioning

João does not talk about AI in theory: he ships products built with AI, solo.
- A YouTube channel (25.5k subscribers, 355 videos, ~880k views) where he builds things live with AI ("vibecoding").
- LobotoMax, his game studio, with real games on Steam (Incremental Vampire, released 23 Jul 2026; Burnout Runs, demo 20 Oct 2026).
- Genie, his own app that produces a YouTube channel's videos end to end: idea, planning, script, narration, thumbnail, plus an ebook and its sales page (example: Código Pug).

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive mostly from WhatsApp shares, LinkedIn, Instagram and QR codes shown at the end of talks; many on phones. Contact happens by WhatsApp.
